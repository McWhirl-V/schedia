Schedia is provided as an independent app for personal use by students. By using it you agree to the following.

1. What Schedia is

Schedia is a personal planning tool. It is not an official service of any institution and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any university, college or timetabling provider. Class times, exam dates, credits and degree requirements shown in Schedia are a copy you imported or entered. Your institution's official records take precedence in all cases.

2. Imported and AI-generated content

Schedia can read a calendar file or feed that you supply, and can parse a reply you obtained from an AI assistant of your own choosing. Schedia does not verify that either is correct, and an AI assistant can misread a timetable. Review the preview before importing, and check anything you rely on.

Schedia does not send your material to an AI service. Any service you choose to use is a separate relationship between you and that provider, on their terms.

3. Your responsibility

You are responsible for the material you import and for keeping your device secure. Do not use Schedia to access a system you are not entitled to access.

4. Availability

Schedia runs on your device and does not depend on a developer-operated service. Features that rely on Apple services (iCloud sync, Apple Maps, notifications) depend on those services being available.

6. Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: no account, no developer server, and data is processed locally, with optional private iCloud sync, user-requested calendar feed retrieval, Apple system services, and exports you choose to share. Card photos never sync.
