A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.

Still wondering? Visit support ↗
How do I bring in my timetable?+

Import an .ics file or a secure calendar subscription link. You can also use AI-assisted import, a schedule code, or enter classes by hand. Always check the preview before importing.

Will my calendar feed refresh automatically?+

No. A calendar feed is fetched only when you ask. Schedia keeps the link for that schedule so you can refresh it again later.

Does Schedia upload my timetable to AI?+

No. It prepares material and instructions for you. You choose an AI assistant, send the material yourself, and paste the reply back into Schedia. Review anything you share.

Can my week start on Sunday?+

Yes. Choose your week start in Settings. Your term start date determines the week number, and breaks follow your institution’s calendar.

Will the GPA match my transcript?+

Only when your GPA rule matches your institution’s. The default US 4.0 scale is a template you can edit or disable. Your official transcript always takes precedence.

How do I delete my data?+

Use Settings → Data to clear selected data or all local data. Delete any iCloud copy separately in your Apple Account’s storage settings. See Privacy choices & deletion for the full steps.
